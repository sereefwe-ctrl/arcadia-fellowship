about arcadia

we are a community for researchers & technical founders from academia and industry. we work on research, open-source projects, and real-world applications to build the next leading AI startups.

we support the next generation leaders with our talents & investor network, mentors, credits & GPUs, and anything else they need along the way. our goal is to help 10 arcadians to build 10 unicorns by 2030.

agenda: talks, retreats and dinners with researchers, founders, mentors and investors. all activities are free, sponsored by our supporters, and we don't take equity.

< benefits of becoming an arcadian >

  • a sponsored retreat to a dedicated country with other arcadians. happening once a quarter, each time in a different country.
  • meet $b+ founders and tier 1 investors from around the world.
  • GPUs and credits supported by our sponsors.
  • access to our network: investors, mentors, talents, and friends.
  • at the end of the day, we want to create a physical and non-physical space for friends. think of it as a group chat with the most talented people on planet Earth.
